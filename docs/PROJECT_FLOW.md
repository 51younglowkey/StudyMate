# Project flow / 项目推进流程

Use this route before proposing the first task or a next move. Material intake
and task selection must sit inside an evidence-led Research and Design process.
An idea, a course PPT or a requested prototype is not proof that research and
analysis have already happened.

## 默认主线 / Default progression

```text
理解目标、课程要求与现状
  → 明确工作问题与调研缺口
  → 规划并开展调研
  → 整理、分析与综合证据
  → 提出洞察、机会与设计标准
  → 探索和比较概念
  → 原型、测试与迭代
```

This is an iterative progression, not a requirement to restart every existing
project or finish a whole phase before doing any useful work. Return to research
when synthesis, concept comparison or testing reveals a missing basis.

| Stage | What to inspect or establish | Useful work now | Basis for the next stage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orientation / framing | User aim, applicable brief, context, current work and uncertainty | Requirements/context map, working question, assumptions and research gaps | A bounded question and a relevant research need |
| Research | Existing literature/observations, missing information, suitable methods and access/consent | Research plan, reading notes, authorised evidence collection or review | Located observations/sources with scope and limitations |
| Analysis / synthesis | Source material, quotations, observations and competing explanations | Separate evidence from interpretation; compare patterns, contradictions and gaps | Traceable candidate insights and remaining uncertainty |
| Opportunity / criteria | Insights, user aim and course constraints | Propose opportunities and design criteria with evidence links | Explicit criteria, assumptions and owner-confirmed decisions where needed |
| Concept exploration | Criteria and supporting evidence | Generate/compare concepts, identify what each assumes | A justified candidate and a defined learning question |
| Prototype / testing / iteration | Concept basis, learning question and prototype/test records | Build a bounded prototype, plan/conduct authorised tests, interpret and iterate | Recorded responses, interpretation, decisions and limits on claimed impact |

Course requirements guide the assignment. Teaching slides guide concepts and
methods. Neither automatically supplies evidence about participants or proves
a design opportunity. Intake alone is not completed research.

## 入口先判断阶段 / Stage-aware entry

1. Understand the user's goal and ask what they have actually done: only an
   idea/course material, some research, analysed findings, concepts, or a
   prototype/test. Reuse answers and relevant authorised records already present.
2. Propose the current stage and explain the observed basis. Unknown progress
   remains unknown; do not infer it from file counts, a polished slide deck or
   the user's preferred output. Follow owner confirmation rules for CURRENT.md.
3. For a new idea or only course/reference material, default to framing and
   research: identify what must be understood, review relevant sources, then
   plan the smallest useful investigation. Do not offer prototype development
   as a peer choice in this beginner intake.
4. For an existing project, enter at the supported stage and check upstream
   evidence links. Missing research calls for a bounded repair, not automatic
   rejection of all existing work or a full restart.
5. Match the first result to that stage. An early result may be a working
   question, source/context review, research gap or research plan. A concept
   comparison/test plan is appropriate only when its inputs exist or it is
   explicitly labelled as a provisional exercise with missing support.

Exploratory prototypes can sometimes serve as research instruments. If used
early, state the research question and what the artifact may help reveal; do
not treat it as a selected solution, completed research or validated impact.

At every handoff, show: proposed/confirmed stage, available basis, missing
connection and one concrete action at that stage. Keep evidence collection,
analysis, design decisions and future proposals visibly separate.
