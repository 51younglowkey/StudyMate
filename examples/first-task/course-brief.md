# Synthetic course brief / 合成课程要求

Status: synthetic example, not an official requirement.

## Assignment

Explore a small service experience for people joining a shared learning space.
Present the research basis, explain a design choice and propose a prototype test.

## Required work

- Frame a working question and distinguish assumptions from observations.
- Connect the chosen concept to source material or clearly state missing evidence.
- Explain what a prototype could help learn and what it could not demonstrate.
- Show an iteration proposal and limitations.

## Teaching context

Journey mapping can organise stages and questions. The map itself does not prove
that a user need exists or that an intervention will work.
