# Synthetic project idea / 合成项目想法

Status: synthetic example. No empirical evidence has been collected.

## Intended outcome

Explore whether a simple welcome card could help a newcomer understand how to
ask for help in a shared learning space.

## Current proposal

Make a card showing where to begin and who might help. This is a design proposal,
not a validated need or proven solution.

## Current uncertainty

We do not know what newcomers find confusing or whether a card is useful.
We want to connect this idea to the synthetic brief and choose a first research task.
